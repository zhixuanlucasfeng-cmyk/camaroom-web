Q: What is a solar panel?

A:

A solar panel (or solar module) is a device which consists of polycrystalline/monocrystalline solar cells, low iron high transmittance anti-reflective tempered glass, encapsulant (like EVA, POE etc...), functional backsheet, PV ribbon, bus bar, junction box and aluminium alloy frame.

Solar panels are a key as well as significant part in a solar power generation system, whose function is to transfer solar energy into electricity to be restored in batteries, or to power the loads.

Solar panels can be classified into following three types :

  1. Polycrystalline silicon solar panel
  2. Monocrystalline silicon solar panel
  3. Amorphous silicon solar panels.

We mainly manufacture polycrystalline silicon and monocrystalline silicon solar panel.

Q: What is the difference between polycrystalline and monocrystalline?

A:

Generally, monocrystalline solar panels have a higher efficiency in converting solar energy into electricity, therefore can reduce more space for you when installation. On the other hand, monocrystalline solar cells are of black color, which makes the whole monocrystalline solar panel more aesthetic.

While polycrystalline solar panels are easier to produce with lower cost, so they are more cost-effective option. In appearance, polycrystalline solar cells are of blue color and sometimes with snowflake pattern.

For solar panels users, there exists less difference between monocrystalline and polycrystalline types, they both have good service life and stability. Customers can choose according to your practical demand.

Q: What is the application range of solar panel?

A:

A solar panel can be applied in a variety range of on-grid and off-grid solar system applications. Typically, there are following application fields:

  1. Residential off-grid solar systems: For rural areas to power like TV, radio, light, water pump.
  2. Residential on-grid rooftop solar systems: Users can gain subsidy by selling the superfuous electricity generated by solar panels to state grid or electric company.
  3. Traffic field: Like beacon light, railway/traffic light..
  4. Tele-communications: Solar unattended microwave relay station, optical cable maintenance station, broadcast/communication/paging power system etc..
  5. Solar power plant: KW-scale and MW-scale solar power plant, various large parking garage charging stations, etc.
  6. BIPV: Combining solar power generation with building materials will enable power generation in future large-scale buildings, which is a major development direction in the future.
  7. Household lighting power supply: Garden lights, street lights, portable lights, camping lights, mountain lights.
  8. Other fields.
