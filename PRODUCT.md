# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Solar buyers in Cameroon, Mali, Nigeria, and Sudan who need equipment for homes, shops, farms, and businesses, often on mobile connections.
- Local country staff who answer customer messages and manage their own country's products, stock, and orders through the separate employee admin.

## Product Purpose

Restar Solar's public website helps a visitor select their country, understand the available solar equipment, contact the correct local team, request a quote, and move from AI assistance to a human employee when needed. Success means the visitor trusts that the company, products, certifications, and local support are real and can reach the right person quickly.

## Positioning

One Restar Solar storefront serves four country operations while routing each customer to the correct local catalogue, inventory, ordering options, language, and human support team.

## Operating Context

Customers may arrive from low-bandwidth mobile networks, compare panels, batteries, inverters, and complete systems, then ask for sizing help or a current quote. Product prices and availability change, so the site should direct customers to confirm them rather than having the AI state a price as fact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Country selection supports Cameroon (CM), Mali (ML), Nigeria (NG), Sudan (SD), and an international fallback.
- The interface supports English, French, and Arabic, including right-to-left layout where appropriate.
- Product catalogue data, country contacts, WhatsApp routes, cart availability, certificates, and human handoff must continue to work.
- The public site and employee admin are separate deployments but one product experience.
- Do not invent prices, availability, warranties, project locations, customers, testimonials, certifications, or performance claims.
- Preserve usable behavior on mobile and slow connections; decorative video must not be required to understand or use the page.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Restar Solar.
- Trust must come from verifiable evidence, direct language, and useful product information—not generated lifestyle imagery, synthetic product renders, decorative AI artwork, or inflated marketing claims.
- Use only authentic company-supplied product, factory, certificate, installation, and project media whose origin can be identified. When authenticity is uncertain, omit the image and use a restrained non-photographic placeholder.
- The site should feel like an established solar equipment and service business, not an AI-generated landing-page template.

## Evidence on Hand

- Restar Solar logo and favicon in the repository.
- Product catalogue records and product photographs under `assets/products/`.
- Certification scans under `assets/certs/`.
- Factory/project gallery under `assets/gallery/` and a small set of farm/project photographs under `assets/farm/`; these require provenance review before prominent use.
- No approved customer testimonials or independently verified case-study copy is currently recorded; do not fabricate either.
- The current hero video and poster are not approved brand evidence and must not remain part of the redesigned experience.

## Product Principles

1. Show proof, not decoration.
2. Make country context and the responsible local team unmistakable.
3. Help customers reach a useful product or a human answer quickly.
4. Never present changing commercial details as guaranteed facts.
5. Keep the experience usable on ordinary phones and constrained networks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, meaningful alternative text, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and correct Arabic directionality.
